Claire Mayfield

Claire Mayfield is a composer and sound designer for video games and film currently studying in Boston, MA. At the Berklee College of Music on a World Tour Scholarship, she is double majoring in Game & Interactive Media Scoring and Film & Media Scoring and minoring in Conducting.

Claire's musical journey began at the age of six, where she began taking violin lessons, immediately falling in love with the instrument. Around the same time, Claire's love for video games and video game music began, as she grew up playing Nintendo 64 titles such as Banjo Kazooie (1998) and Mario Kart 64 (1996). At the age of eight, Claire began composing music, with her first piece titled Violin Duet in A Major.

When Claire was twelve years old, she discovered a DAW for the first time by stumbling across Garageband on her phone. She learned how to use the software, and began to create and record her own arrangements and compositions. Over the years, she also began to play violin for movies and TV, playing in titles such as Tim Timmerman, Hope of America (2017) and the Apple TV show Stillwater (2020). It was through these recording sessions that she was able to see the process of film scoring up close for the first time.

Throughout high school, Claire continued her studies in violin, performing as a soloist three times in Olympus High School's Concerto Night, and playing as the concertmaster for the Young Artist Chamber Players. At this time, she also took AP Music Theory, where she formally studied composition for the first time. For her final project, she rescored a scene from the TV series Miraculous Ladybug (2015). Her love and passion for this project was the start of her shift from a focus in violin performance to composition.

In 2023, Claire auditioned for the Berklee College of Music and was accepted with a full-tuition scholarship. At this time, she was also awarded the Christensen Concerto Scholarship for her solo concerto performances at Olympus High School.

Since beginning her studies at Berklee, Claire has gained leadership experience as a concertmaster for the Berklee World Strings and Audire Soundtrack Collective, and as an assistant concertmaster for the Berklee Contemporary Symphony Orchestra and the Berklee Motion Picture Orchestra. Claire is also the PR Manager for Audire, managing publicity for the ensemble and working as the collective's graphic designer. With a high dedication to her studies, she has placed on the Dean's List for every semester she has attended.

In spring 2025, Claire worked as the sound designer for the video game Lost in an Illusion, as part of a collaboration project with USC students. She is also currently developing her own game titled Persuit of Love, working as a composer, sound designer, programmer, and audio implementer. Claire has also composed for two concerts for the Berklee World Strings and arranges video game soundtracks for the Audire Soundtrack Collective.

In addition to her studies and ensembles at Berklee, Claire also works as a session musician, playing violin in student compositions for film, TV, and video games. Claire has loved her time at Berklee so far, and is excited for what her future holds.

With a strong passion for music, video games, film, and TV, Claire loves working with others and is always open to meeting new people to collaborate on projects.
