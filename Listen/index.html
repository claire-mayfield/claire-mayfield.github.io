UNMASCED



Developed for the 2026 Global Game Jam at Northeastern University, Unmasced is a 2D platformer about crashing a royal ball, and discovering secrets about both the kingdom and yourself. You traverse through obstacles and collect items that allow you to put on different masks, allowing you to go deeper into the castle.

All of the music in this demo is composed and implemented by me.
The sound design is by Lizzie Erin, along with music in other parts of the game.

The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild - Tower
(Wwise Randomization Demo and Rescore)



This is a rescore of the Tower theme from Breath of the Wild, and a demonstration of how it works within Wwise middleware. It utilizes branching and randomization of small phrases in order to give the player a new experience every time they visit the tower. Because the order is randomized, they will never hear it in the same way twice!
