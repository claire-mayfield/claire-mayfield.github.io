Audire Soundtrack Collective

Audire Soundtrack Collective is a student-run ensemble at the Berklee College of Music in Boston, MA that performs music from video games, anime, film and TV. The music is entirely arranged, performed, and conducted by Berklee students.

The collective, which has over 75 members, includes an orchestra, choir, and smaller ensembles such as Audire Unplugged and Jazztaku.
